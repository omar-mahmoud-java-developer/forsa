# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be respectful, welcoming and constructive; no harassment or discrimination; assume good faith; give and accept feedback
gracefully. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's Security tab (private report) or the
contact in the repository profile. Maintainers may remove content and ban contributors who violate these rules.
