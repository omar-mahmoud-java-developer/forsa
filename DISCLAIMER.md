**Automating LinkedIn may violate LinkedIn's User Agreement and can get your account restricted or closed.**
This project is provided as-is, for personal use. You are solely responsible for how you use it,
for complying with the terms of every site you connect it to, and for the applications and messages
that are sent in your name.

- Start with `dry_run: true` (the default) and read the results in `output/digest.html`.
- Keep limits low (`max_applications_per_day`, `max_connects_per_day`), and prefer a secondary account.
- Nothing is applied, connected or emailed until you switch the safety settings yourself.
- Answers to application questions come only from your `profile.md`. If you write something untrue there,
